// ABOUT

What is Cyber Fraud?

Definition

Cyber fraud refers to illegal activities carried out through computers, mobile devices, digital platforms, or communication networks in order to steal money, data, identity, or emotional security from users.

How It Works

Modern cyber criminals often do not break into systems by force. Instead, they misuse human behavior — creating fear, urgency, curiosity, greed, or trust to convince people to reveal information or transfer money.

Who Is at Risk?

School students, college students, elderly people, shopkeepers, salaried employees, and homemakers can all become victims because fraudsters target human habits rather than only technical weaknesses.

A single careless action — sharing an OTP, clicking a fake link, or trusting a duplicate account — can lead to financial loss, emotional stress, and identity misuse.

Major Cyber Threats

OTP Fraud

Fraudsters trick you into sharing your One-Time Password to perform unauthorized transactions.

OTP Fraud

  • KYC update scams
  • UPI collect request scams
  • SIM swap fraud
  • Remote access fraud
No genuine authority ever asks for your OTP

Social Media Scams

Fraudsters use fake profiles on Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp to steal money or personal info.

Social Media Scams

  • Romance scams
  • Lottery / prize scams
  • Fake job offers
  • Investment fraud
Verification must come before trust

// HOW THEY TRICK YOU

The Psychology of Cyber Fraud

FEAR

"Your account will be blocked!"

URGENCY

"Share OTP now or lose access!"

TRUST

"I'm calling from your bank..."

GREED

"You've won ₹10 lakh lottery!"

SYMPATHY

"I'm your friend, I need help urgently!"

CURIOSITY

"Click to see who viewed your profile!"

A calm response is the strongest defense. Stop. Think. Verify.

// PREVENTION

How to Stay Safe

Use strong, unique passwords for every account
Enable two-factor authentication everywhere
Never share OTP, PIN, or CVV with anyone
Avoid clicking unknown or suspicious links
Keep devices and apps updated regularly
Avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive transactions
Install apps only from official stores
Enable transaction alerts on your accounts
Use only official banking apps and websites
Remember: receiving money never needs a PIN
Get customer care numbers from official bank website only
Lock cards immediately if anything suspicious appears
Review account statements regularly
Remove screen-sharing apps from banking devices
Set spending limits on cards where possible
Read every payment request carefully before approving
Verify profiles before accepting requests
Call the person directly before sending money
Use strong privacy settings on all platforms
Block and report fake accounts immediately
Limit public posting of personal details
Verify before trusting any online message
Don't respond angrily to cyberbullying
Save screenshots as evidence of harassment
Share documents only through trusted channels
Shred physical copies of sensitive documents
Mask unnecessary details when sharing documents
Lock your devices with strong screen locks
Monitor email and financial alerts regularly
Review social media privacy settings often
Minimize data shared on online forms
Act immediately on unknown OTPs or loan notices
// REAL CASES

Case Studies

OTP Fraud

"A working professional received a call from someone claiming to be a bank representative. The caller said urgent KYC verification was needed and asked for the OTP. Trusting the caller, the victim shared the code — and within minutes, a large amount was deducted from the account."

An OTP is a key to your account. Handing it to another person is equal to handing over control of the account itself.
Social Media Scam

"A student received an Instagram message from an account that looked exactly like a close friend's profile. The sender said there was an urgent financial problem and requested money immediately. The student transferred the amount and later discovered the real friend had sent no such message."

Not everything visible on social media is real. Always call and verify before sending money to anyone online.
// REPORT CYBER CRIME

How to Report

Cyber Crime Helpline

1930

Call immediately if you are a victim of cyber fraud. Available 24/7 across India.

Online Portal

cybercrime.gov.in

File a detailed complaint online through the official Government of India cyber crime portal.

Contact Your Bank

Immediately

Inform your bank, card issuer, and relevant platforms without delay to freeze transactions.

Steps to Take After Cyber Fraud

1

Stay calm and stop all transactions

2

Change all passwords immediately

3

Inform your bank & block cards

4

Document everything (screenshots)

5

Call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in
